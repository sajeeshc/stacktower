Privacy Policy for Stack Tower

Last updated: October 2026

Thank you for playing Stack Tower ("the Game"). Your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles your data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Stack Tower does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

All game data (such as your current high score, sound settings, and maximum level achieved) is stored strictly locally on your device using standard offline local storage. None of this data is ever sent to our servers or third parties.

2. Internet and Network Access

Stack Tower does not require an active internet connection to play and does not communicate with external analytics or tracking servers.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The game currently contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party telemetry tools.

4. Children’s Privacy

Stack Tower does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected from any users, our game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR regulations.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
